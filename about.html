Our story

Roasted with care. Served with calm.

Bean & Bloom began as a neighborhood espresso counter and grew into a specialty cafe and shop for people who want better coffee at the bar and at home.

Crafted for daily ritual

We source seasonal lots, roast in small batches, and train every barista on dial-in, milk texture, and hospitality. The goal is simple: a cup that feels intentional, whether you stay for twenty minutes or take beans home for the week.

Alongside drinks, our shop carries brew gear, gift boxes, and bakery made fresh each morning — so one stop covers your cafe visit and your home setup.

Thoughtful sourcing

We work with importers who share farm details, processing notes, and roast-ready profiles we can stand behind.

Warm service

Ask for a recommendation, a grind setting, or a quieter table — the team is here to make the visit easy.

Cafe + home

Drink in, take away, or shop beans and tools for mornings that start the same way ours do.
