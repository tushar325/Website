Privacy Policy

How Bean & Bloom collects, uses, and protects information when you shop, create an account, or contact support.

Effective: 1 August 2026 Card data is never stored by Bean & Bloom
On this page
What we collect How we use data Payments Storage & cookies Sharing Your choices Contact

1. What we collect

Depending on how you use the shop, we may process:

  • Account details such as name, email, and optional phone number
  • Order and delivery addresses
  • Support messages sent through Contact or WhatsApp chat
  • Basic site preferences stored in your browser (theme, cart, recently viewed)

2. How we use data

We use information to fulfill orders, provide account access, improve the storefront, prevent fraud, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Payments

Online payments run through Razorpay Checkout. Card numbers and CVV are entered with Razorpay and are not stored by Bean & Bloom. Cash on Delivery orders collect only the contact and address details needed for delivery.

4. Storage & cookies

Some preferences and session data are kept in your browser (for example cart contents and sign-in session). Server-side order records, when enabled, store order and fulfillment details needed to run the shop. Session chat messages in the on-site WhatsApp popup are kept only for the browser session.

5. Sharing

We may share limited data with payment processors, shipping partners, and infrastructure providers solely to operate the store. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Your choices

You can update account details, sign out, clear browser storage, or ask us to help with access or deletion requests related to your account and orders where applicable.

7. Contact

Privacy questions? Use the Contact page. Related terms are in our Terms of Service.
